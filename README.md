## 👋 Who we are

Ordnexa helps small businesses turn **messy messages, admin work and forgotten follow-ups** into structured, reliable systems.

The name is the idea:

> **ORD** = *Ordnung* (order) · **NEX** = *nexus* (connection) · **A** = *automation, agents, assistants*

## 🔥 The problem we solve

Small businesses don't lose customers because their work is bad. They lose them in the gaps between their tools:

- 📱 an inquiry arrives while the owner is busy with a customer
- ⏰ a follow-up never gets sent
- 🗂️ information is scattered across chats, mailboxes and spreadsheets

We close those gaps.

## 🛠️ What we build

| | |
|---|---|
| 💬 **Lead recovery** | Capture every inquiry from WhatsApp, web forms and email. Answer fast. Follow up on open requests. |
| 🔗 **Workflow automation** | Connect the tools a business already uses and remove repetitive manual steps. |
| 🤖 **AI assistants with limits** | Assistants that stay inside a defined scope, say clearly that they are AI, and hand over to a human when it matters. |
| 🔔 **Owner visibility** | The owner is alerted only when a person is needed, and can see what happened, with no new platform to learn. |

## 🌟 Vision

We want automation in small businesses to be **calm, understandable and trustworthy.**

- 🧱 **Order before speed.** Fix the process first, then automate it.
- 🧑‍💼 **Humans stay in control.** AI handles repetition. People make judgement calls, approve sensitive actions and own the customer relationship.
- 🔒 **Privacy by design.** Only the data a workflow needs, kept for a defined time, covered by data-processing agreements. EU-hosted setups wherever the tooling allows.
- 🔓 **No lock-in.** Customers keep their accounts, numbers and data. We work as a partner and can hand everything over.
- 🔬 **Small, tested, observable.** Narrow scope, written acceptance tests, monitoring, and a documented way to switch things off.

## ⚙️ How we work

1. 🔍 **Review:** a short look at how inquiries and follow-ups work today.
2. 🧪 **Pilot:** a small trial with clear success measures, before any long commitment.
3. 🏗️ **Build:** open, inspectable tooling such as [n8n](https://n8n.io), with secrets kept in credential stores and never in code.
4. 📝 **Document:** everything we build is written down so the customer understands it.
